## 🛠️ Colophon {#colophon}

I started this website in October 2025 when I just came to second year. The reason why this website is created is that I want to improve my present skills, writing abilities in Englsih and the most important is to archieve my knowledge about what I have learned as a journey.

I chose `Hugo` to create a website since it is convenient and I can write Latex code in Markdown. Therefore, I searched for many website and I decided to choose a template from [`Henrique Dias`](https://hacdias.com) because I was surprised by the idea and content he made for his website which is fit for me. All of the color and structure of this website come from his website and I adjust many things to appropriate for my self.
