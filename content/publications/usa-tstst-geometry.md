## Abstract

In this paper, we present several extensions to a beautiful and important problem from
the 2016 USA TSTST. This problem itself serves as an extremely interesting illustration
of the method using power of a point and the radical axis in plane geometry. In our proof for the extensions problems, in addition to combining the method of power of a point and the radical axis, we also utilize the cross ratio of points and pencils of lines along with central projection, which are important tools of projective geometry.
