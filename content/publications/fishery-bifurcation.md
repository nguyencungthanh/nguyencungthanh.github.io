## Abstract

We analyze a generalized fishery model incorporating fast-slow dynamics and aggregation methods. Using bifurcation theory, we characterize the equilibrium structure and stability properties of the reduced system, providing insights into sustainable yield management under ecological constraints.

## My Contributions

- Proved the main theorem on Maximum Sustainable Yield (MSY) in the generalized logistic growth model.
- Analyzed equilibrium points and their stability properties.
- Performed bifurcation analysis on the reduced system.
- Implemented Maple simulations for visualization and numerical verification.
